Voltage to Current Formula

Ohm's Law relates voltage, current, and resistance. When voltage and resistance are known, current is:

I = V ÷ R

Where:

  • I = current in amps
  • V = voltage in volts
  • R = resistance in ohms

Example

A 24 VDC control circuit has a resistance of 1,200 Ω.

I = 24 ÷ 1200 = 0.020 A

The current is: 20 mA

24 VDC Controls Applications

Ohm's Law is frequently useful when troubleshooting control circuits, relays, solenoids, sensors, and signal conditioning devices.

Example: Control Load

If a 24 VDC source is connected to a 600 Ω load:

I = 24 ÷ 600 = 0.040 A = 40 mA

This can help determine whether a power supply or control output is being asked to provide more current than expected.

Troubleshooting

Measured current is lower than expected

Check the actual supply voltage, circuit resistance, wiring connections, and whether additional resistance has been introduced into the circuit.

Measured current is higher than expected

Check for a resistance value lower than expected, unintended parallel paths, wiring faults, or a load that differs from the assumed circuit.

Voltage is correct but the device does not operate

Measure voltage under load rather than only with the circuit disconnected. A supply can show the expected voltage with no load but experience voltage drop when current is flowing.

Common Mistakes

  • Entering resistance in kilohms while treating it as ohms.
  • Forgetting that 1 A = 1000 mA.
  • Using open-circuit voltage instead of the voltage actually present across the load.
  • Ignoring additional series resistance.
  • Forgetting that parallel loads change total circuit resistance.

Engineering Note

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance. Actual control circuits can include nonlinear devices, current-limiting circuits, power supplies, and other components that do not behave like a simple resistor.

Use this calculator as a basic electrical reference and verify actual circuit behavior with appropriate field measurements and equipment documentation.
